#  AI Prompt Library

## AI-Driven E-Commerce Analytics

This document contains the prompts used throughout the project to demonstrate how Generative AI can automate and enhance the end-to-end data analytics workflow.

---

# 📌 Prompt 1 – Business Understanding

### Objective

Understand the business context before starting the analysis.

### Prompt

```text
You are a Senior Data Analyst.

I have an e-commerce dataset.

Analyze the dataset and explain:

- Business domain
- Business model
- Target customers
- Business objectives
- Possible KPIs
- Business problems that can be solved using this dataset
- Potential business questions to answer

Present your response in a structured business report.
```
